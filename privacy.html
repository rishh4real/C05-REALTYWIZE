Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

At Realtywize A Realty Co., your privacy matters. We are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your information when you visit realtywize.co, submit an enquiry, contact us, or otherwise interact with our real estate services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, mobile number, city or location, property preferences, budget range, preferred property type, enquiry details, and information shared during calls, WhatsApp conversations, site visits, or other communications.

We may also collect technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, operating system, pages visited, visit date and time, referral source, website usage data, cookies, and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, recommend suitable properties, arrange site visits or consultations, contact you through permitted communication channels, share relevant project or property information, provide customer support, improve our website and services, maintain business records, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent using the contact details below, subject to applicable law.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Where reasonably necessary, we may share relevant information with property developers, builders, promoters, property owners, authorised real estate representatives, service providers, professional advisers, regulators, courts, or competent authorities.

We seek to disclose only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand usage, remember preferences, improve functionality, and measure performance. You may restrict cookies through your browser settings, although some website functionality may be affected.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with applicable law.

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable Indian law, you may request access to information about personal data being processed, correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, erasure where applicable, withdrawal of consent where processing is consent-based, or raise a grievance regarding the handling of your information.

7. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to property developers, payment providers, social-media platforms, map services, or other third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, accuracy, or content of third-party websites.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for persons legally capable of entering into property-related transactions. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children except where permitted by applicable law and subject to required parental or guardian consent.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, business practices, technology, or applicable law. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact and Grievances

For privacy-related questions, requests, complaints, or withdrawal of consent, contact Realtywize A Realty Co. at 1615B Wave One, Silver Wing, Sector 18, Noida 201301, or call +91 98919 00500.
